Posture & Alignment

1. Choose view

Take one photo per view, with the camera level and the child standing naturally. For better repeatability, palpate landmarks and place a small, high-contrast skin marker at each location before photographing, where possible. Centre each virtual dot on its physical marker. Front pelvis: left/right ASIS. Back pelvis: left/right PSIS. Side neck: C7 spinous process tip and centre of tragus. Keep the camera level.

3. Tap landmarks

Recommended: use small, contrasting skin markers over palpated bony landmarks (including C7, ASIS, PSIS and the tibial tubercle). Place each virtual dot at the marker centre. The next landmark highlights automatically. Tap the photo to place it. To correct any marker at any time, press directly on its dot and drag it. You can also select its name and tap a new position. The measurements update automatically.

Rotation is an approximate visual correction and does not prove the camera was level. Recheck measurements after changing rotation.
